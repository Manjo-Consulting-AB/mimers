# Containers and items

## How do I organise my items?

There are several ways to organise information in Mimers, and you can combine them:

- **Hierarchy.** Place items inside other items. For example, a camera bag can contain a camera, lenses and a charger.
- **Categories and tags.** Create your own categories and tags to group and classify items in whatever way makes sense to you.
- **Relations.** Link items that belong together without placing one inside the other, such as a fuse and the pump it protects.

You decide how to organise your information. Mimers doesn't impose a predefined structure.

## Can I add my own fields to items?

Yes. You can define up to 25 custom fields per container to store information such as serial numbers, purchase dates, manufacturers or anything else relevant to your needs.

You can choose between three field types:

- **Text** – for names, descriptions and other text.
- **Number** – for quantities, measurements and amounts.
- **Date** – for dates such as purchases, inspections or renewals.

Fields are configured at container level and can be used by items within that container. You can also use them to sort and filter your items.

## Can I save and reuse my own templates?

Yes. You can create and save templates for both custom fields and categories.

Templates are saved to your account and can be reused whenever you create a new container. This makes it easy to apply the same structure across multiple containers without having to set everything up again.

## How do I find something quickly?

Use search to find items across your containers. You can also narrow down the results using categories, tags and custom fields.

## Can I move an item to another place?

Yes. You can move an item under another parent within the same container. All its child items move with it, so you don't lose the structure you've created.

## Can I keep track of what I've lent out?

Yes. You can mark an item as lent and record who borrowed it. Reminders about loans are available with Pro and Pro+.

## Can I record what things cost?

Yes. You can record costs on individual items on every plan, and attach receipts if you like.

Pro and Pro+ also include cost reports that help you see the costs across an entire container.

## I deleted something by mistake. Can I get it back?

Yes. Deleted items, attachments and containers are kept in the trash for 30 days. You can restore them at any time during that period. After 30 days, they are permanently deleted.
