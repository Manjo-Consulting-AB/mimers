# Plans and pricing

## What does Mimers cost?

Mimers offers three plans. Pro and Pro+ include the same features. The only difference is the amount of storage.

| | Free | Pro | Pro+ |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| **Price per year** | €0 | €39 | €69 |
| **Founder price per year** | — | €19 | €39 |
| Containers | 1 | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| Storage | 1 GB | 10 GB | 50 GB |
| Maximum file size | 10 MB | 64 MB | 64 MB |
| People to share with per container | 1 | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| Webhooks | — | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| MCP access | — | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| Priority support | — | Yes | Yes |

All prices are annual. We do not offer monthly subscriptions.

## What's included in every plan?

The following features are available on all plans:

- Tasks and reminders.
- Personal calendar links.
- Cost records on items.
- Export of your container data.

Your data is never held hostage behind a subscription. You can export your containers regardless of your plan.

## What's the difference between Pro and Pro+?

Pro and Pro+ include exactly the same features. The only difference is storage capacity:

- **Pro:** 10 GB.
- **Pro+:** 50 GB.

Both plans include unlimited containers, unlimited sharing, webhooks, MCP access and priority support.

## What's the Founder price?

Anyone who subscribes during the first twelve months after payments open gets access to our special Founder price:

- **Pro:** €19 per year.
- **Pro+:** €39 per year.

Your Founder price stays the same for as long as your subscription remains active. If your subscription ends, you lose the Founder price and will need to pay the current regular price if you subscribe again.

## Will the price go up?

Prices may change over time, but an active subscription keeps its current price.

Any price changes apply only to new subscriptions. You won't pay more simply because we increase our prices.

## What happens if I downgrade my plan?

Your Pro or Pro+ features are disabled immediately when your subscription is downgraded.

If your data fits within the limits of your new plan, nothing else happens. For example, moving from Pro+ to Pro is straightforward if you're using less than 10 GB.

If your account exceeds the new plan's limits, it enters read-only mode for up to three months.

During this period, you can:

- View your data.
- Delete attachments.
- Empty the trash.
- Export your information.

You cannot create or edit data or use paid features.

You have three months to bring your account within the new plan's limits. If you don't, Mimers automatically removes data in the following order:

1. **Attachments:** The newest attachments are deleted first until your storage usage fits the new plan's limit.
2. **Containers:** If you still have more containers than your new plan allows, the newest containers are deleted until you are within the limit.

For example, when moving to Free, your account must fit within 1 GB of storage and one container.

We send reminders one month and one week before automatic deletion begins.

## What happens when my subscription expires?

When your subscription expires, your account moves to the Free plan.

Your Pro or Pro+ features are disabled immediately. If your account exceeds the Free plan's limits, the same three-month transition period applies.

You can use this time to delete attachments and export your data. After three months, Mimers automatically deletes the newest attachments first, followed by the newest containers if necessary, until your account fits within the Free plan.

## Will my items and information be deleted if I downgrade?

No. Your items and their information are not deleted simply because you downgrade.

Only attachments may be automatically removed to meet your new storage limit. If you have too many containers, the newest containers will also be removed after the storage limit has been met.
