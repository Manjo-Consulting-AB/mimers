# Privacy and your data

## Where is my data stored?

Your data is hosted by Inleed, with data centres in Sweden and France. Backups are made daily.

## Where is my data processed?

Data stored in Mimers is hosted within the EU, in Sweden and France.

If you choose to connect an external AI agent, some of your data may be sent to and processed by the AI provider. Depending on the provider you choose, this processing may take place outside the EU.

Please refer to the AI provider's own privacy policy and data processing terms for details.

## Who can access my files?

Only Mimers system administrators have the technical ability to access stored files. All such access is logged.

Data centre personnel at our hosting provider, Inleed, may also have technical access to system files. This is governed by our agreements with the provider.

## Do you sell my data or show ads?

No. Mimers is funded by subscriptions. We don't show ads or sell your data.

## Can I export my data?

Yes. Data export is available on every plan.

Each container has an **Export** function that creates a ZIP archive containing all data in that container at the time of export, including:

- Items and their data
- Categories and tags
- Attachments
- History

You can download the archive when it's ready and take your data with you at any time.

## Who can see my containers?

Your containers are private by default. Only you and people you explicitly share them with can access them.

Nothing in Mimers is publicly accessible.
