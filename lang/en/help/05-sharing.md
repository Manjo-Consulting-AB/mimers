# Sharing

## How do I share a container?

Open the container, go to **Sharing** and invite someone by email. If they don't have a Mimers account yet, they can create one using that email address and then accept the invitation.

You can share an entire container or give someone access to a specific item and everything inside it.

## What can the people I share with do?

You choose an access level for each person. The levels are cumulative, meaning each level includes the permissions of the previous ones.

- **Read:** Can view everything within their access scope, but cannot make changes.
- **Add:** Can create new items and add attachments, costs and tasks to existing items. They cannot edit or delete existing information.
- **Edit:** Can also edit existing information, including item details and task information.
- **Delete:** Can also delete and restore items, attachments, costs and tasks within their access scope.

To complete, skip or reopen a task, the person needs **Edit** access.

## Can I share just part of a container?

Yes. You can invite someone to an entire container or to an individual item.

When you share an individual item, the person can see that item and its child items, but nothing else in the container. Their permissions apply only within that scope.

## Can I change or revoke someone's access?

Yes. As the container owner, you can change someone's access level or revoke their access at any time.

People you've invited can also choose to leave a shared container or item.

## How many people can I share with?

- **Free:** One person per container.
- **Pro and Pro+:** Unlimited people per container.

## Can I hand a container over to someone else?

Yes. With **Ownership transfer**, you can transfer a container to another Mimers user. This is useful when selling a boat, car or house, or when handing over a project to a customer.

The new owner must accept the transfer. Once accepted, the container and its contents become theirs, including items, attachments, tasks, costs and categories.

Ownership transfer is available with Pro and Pro+.

## What happens to my access when I transfer ownership?

You automatically lose ownership and access to the container after the transfer.

If you need to remain involved, the new owner can invite you back with the access level they choose. For example, a boatyard could retain Edit access after handing a boat over to its new owner.

## What happens to other people who have access to the container?

Existing sharing permissions and pending invitations are not transferred. They are revoked when ownership changes.

The new owner can decide who should have access and invite those people again.

## Can I keep some information when transferring a container?

Yes. You can choose to keep selected items instead of transferring them. These items are moved into a separate container that remains yours.

Links and relationships between items that end up in different containers cannot be retained. Some tags may also no longer apply.

## What happens to deleted items and version history?

The container's trash is permanently emptied as part of the transfer. Items that were already in the trash remain deleted and cannot be restored by the new owner.

Item version history is not transferred.

## Does the new owner need a specific plan?

The recipient must have room for the container under their plan.

A Free account can own one container. If the recipient already has a container, they need to upgrade to Pro or Pro+ before accepting another one.

## Does the new owner get a Mimers subscription?

If the recipient is completely new to Mimers and has no existing account, they receive **12 months of Pro** when accepting an ownership transfer.

This offer is available when the transfer is made by either a Pro or Pro+ user. It is part of our marketing programme to introduce Mimers to people taking over a boat, car, house or project.

The offer is limited to two new-user bonuses per sender in any 12-month period. Existing Mimers users can still accept ownership transfers, but they do not receive an extension to their subscription.
