# Account and security

## How do I log in without a password?

Choose to log in with a link. We'll email you a single-use login link that is valid for 15 minutes.

Mimers currently supports email-based login only. Google and other external login providers are not available.

## How do I turn on two-factor authentication?

Go to **Settings → Security** and set up an authenticator app.

Once enabled, you'll receive ten recovery codes. Store them somewhere safe — they're your backup if you lose access to your authenticator app.

You can generate a new set of recovery codes at any time. Doing so invalidates all previously generated codes.

## How do I change my email address?

Go to **Settings → Profile** and request an email change.

You'll need to confirm the change through a link sent to your new email address. Your current address will remain in place until you complete the confirmation.

For security, we'll also send a notification to your current email address when a change has been requested.

## How do I delete my account?

Go to **Settings → Security** and request account deletion. We'll send you a confirmation link by email.

Nothing is deleted until you open that link and confirm. Once confirmed, the deletion is permanent and all your data is removed.

If you own containers that other people use, you can transfer ownership before confirming deletion. Otherwise, those containers and their data will also be deleted, including data added by other members. Anyone who had access will lose it.

## What happens if I stop using Mimers?

Mimers has an inactivity policy to keep unused accounts from remaining in the system indefinitely.

- **After 12 months:** We'll send you a reminder to use your account.
- **After 15 months:** Your account is closed. Your data is retained.
- **After 18 months:** Your account and its data are permanently deleted.

Any activity on your account resets the inactivity period. Accounts with an active subscription are exempt from inactivity-related closure and deletion.
