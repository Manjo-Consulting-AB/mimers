Yes. You can record money going out and coming in on individual items on every plan, and attach receipts if you like. Each entry is either an expense or an income, and can name who you paid or who paid you.

Three examples:

- **An engine service** is an expense on the boat or the car.
- **A customer you invoice** is an income on the item the work was done for.
- **A share you buy and sell** is an expense when you buy and an income when you sell, each with the number of shares as the quantity.

A credit invoice is recorded as a negative expense.

Pro and Pro+ also include reports that help you see expenses, income and the net across an entire container.
