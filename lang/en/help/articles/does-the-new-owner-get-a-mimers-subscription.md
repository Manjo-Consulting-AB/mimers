If the recipient is completely new to Mimers and has no existing account, they receive **12 months of Pro** when accepting an ownership transfer.

This offer is available when the transfer is made by either a Pro or Pro+ user. It is part of our marketing programme to introduce Mimers to people taking over a boat, car, house or project.

The offer is limited to two new-user bonuses per sender in any 12-month period. Existing Mimers users can still accept ownership transfers, but they do not receive an extension to their subscription.
