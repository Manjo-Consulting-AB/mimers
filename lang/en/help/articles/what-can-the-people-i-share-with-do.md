You choose an access level for each person. The levels are cumulative, meaning each level includes the permissions of the previous ones.

- **Read:** Can view everything within their access scope, but cannot make changes.
- **Add:** Can create new items and add attachments, costs and tasks to existing items. They cannot edit or delete existing information.
- **Edit:** Can also edit existing information, including item details and task information.
- **Delete:** Can also delete and restore items, attachments, costs and tasks within their access scope.

To complete, skip or reopen a task, the person needs **Edit** access.
