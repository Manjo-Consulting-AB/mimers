Yes. Each container has a personal **Calendar** link that you can subscribe to in Google Calendar, Apple Calendar, Outlook or any other calendar app that supports calendar subscriptions.

Tasks with an assigned date appear in your calendar. Recurring tasks are added as they become due or when the previous occurrence has been handled, rather than generating an endless series of future events.

The calendar only shows tasks and information you have access to. Calendar subscriptions are free on every plan.
