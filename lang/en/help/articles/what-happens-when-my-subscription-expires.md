When your subscription expires, your account moves to the Free plan.

Your Pro or Pro+ features are disabled immediately. If your account exceeds the Free plan's limits, the same three-month transition period applies.

You can use this time to delete attachments and export your data. After three months, Mimers automatically deletes the newest attachments first, followed by the newest containers if necessary, until your account fits within the Free plan.
