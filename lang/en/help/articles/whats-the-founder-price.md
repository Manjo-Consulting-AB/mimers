Anyone who subscribes during the first twelve months after payments open gets access to our special Founder price:

- **Pro:** €19 per year.
- **Pro+:** €39 per year.

Your Founder price stays the same for as long as your subscription remains active. If your subscription ends, you lose the Founder price and will need to pay the current regular price if you subscribe again.
