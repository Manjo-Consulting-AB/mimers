Yes. Everything in the trash continues to count towards your storage allowance until the trash is emptied.

You can empty the trash yourself to free up space immediately.

Each container has its own trash, where you can find everything deleted from that container. The system-wide trash brings together deleted items from all containers where you have permission to access the trash.

For security reasons, AI agents cannot empty the trash on your behalf.
