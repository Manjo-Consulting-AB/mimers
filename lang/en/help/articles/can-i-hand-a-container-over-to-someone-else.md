Yes. With **Ownership transfer**, you can transfer a container to another Mimers user. This is useful when selling a boat, car or house, or when handing over a project to a customer.

The new owner must accept the transfer. Once accepted, the container and its contents become theirs, including items, attachments, tasks, costs and categories.

Ownership transfer is available with Pro and Pro+.
