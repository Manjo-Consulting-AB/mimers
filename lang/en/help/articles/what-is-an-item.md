An item is anything you want to keep track of within a container. It could be a physical object, such as an engine, a heat pump or a piece of furniture, but also something less tangible, like a document, a person or an activity.

Items can contain text, photos, files, categories, tags, tasks and costs. You can also link related items together or organise them in a hierarchy, placing smaller items inside larger ones.

For example, an engine can contain its individual parts, just as the engine itself belongs to a boat.
