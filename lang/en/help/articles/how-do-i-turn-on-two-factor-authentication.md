Go to **Settings → Security** and set up an authenticator app.

Once enabled, you'll receive ten recovery codes. Store them somewhere safe — they're your backup if you lose access to your authenticator app.

You can generate a new set of recovery codes at any time. Doing so invalidates all previously generated codes.
