Go to **Settings → Security** and request account deletion. We'll send you a confirmation link by email.

Nothing is deleted until you open that link and confirm. Once confirmed, the deletion is permanent and all your data is removed.

If you own containers that other people use, you can transfer ownership before confirming deletion. Otherwise, those containers and their data will also be deleted, including data added by other members. Anyone who had access will lose it.
