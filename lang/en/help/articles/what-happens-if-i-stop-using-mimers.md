Mimers has an inactivity policy to keep unused accounts from remaining in the system indefinitely.

- **After 12 months:** We'll send you a reminder to use your account.
- **After 15 months:** Your account is closed. Your data is retained.
- **After 18 months:** Your account and its data are permanently deleted.

Any activity on your account resets the inactivity period. Accounts with an active subscription are exempt from inactivity-related closure and deletion.
