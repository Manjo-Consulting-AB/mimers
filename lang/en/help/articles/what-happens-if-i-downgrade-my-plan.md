Your Pro or Pro+ features are disabled immediately when your subscription is downgraded.

If your data fits within the limits of your new plan, nothing else happens. For example, moving from Pro+ to Pro is straightforward if you're using less than 10 GB.

If your account exceeds the new plan's limits, it enters read-only mode for up to three months.

During this period, you can:

- View your data.
- Delete attachments.
- Empty the trash.
- Export your information.

You cannot create or edit data or use paid features.

You have three months to bring your account within the new plan's limits. If you don't, Mimers automatically removes data in the following order:

1. **Attachments:** The newest attachments are deleted first until your storage usage fits the new plan's limit.
2. **Containers:** If you still have more containers than your new plan allows, the newest containers are deleted until you are within the limit.

For example, when moving to Free, your account must fit within 1 GB of storage and one container.

We send reminders one month and one week before automatic deletion begins.
