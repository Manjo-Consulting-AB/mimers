# Tasks and reminders

Mimers uses the GTD (Getting Things Done) approach to help you organise and keep track of everything that needs to be done. You can manage tasks at account, container and item level.

## What is the Inbox?

The Inbox is your personal place to capture tasks as they come to mind, without having to decide where they belong straight away.

You can create tasks directly in your Inbox without linking them to an item. You can also choose to manage and complete tasks directly from the Inbox.

When you're ready to organise a task further, you can move it out of the Inbox and link it to an item.

## Do tasks always have to be linked to an item?

No. Tasks can be managed directly in your Inbox without being linked to an item.

Once you move a task out of the Inbox, it needs to be linked to an item. This keeps tasks connected to the things and information they relate to.

## How do I set up recurring maintenance?

Add a task to an item and choose how often it should repeat. Mimers keeps track of when it's due and reminds you when it's time to do it.

## What's the difference between the two types of repeating tasks?

Mimers supports two ways of scheduling recurring tasks:

- **On fixed dates.** The task follows the calendar, regardless of when you actually complete it. For example, insurance that renews on 1 January every year, even if you pay it late.
- **After completion.** The next due date is calculated from when you last completed the task. For example, an oil change every twelve months after the previous oil change.

You can also create one-off tasks that are completed once and don't repeat.

## Where do I see what needs doing?

The **Tasks** page gives you an overview of tasks across your account, including tasks in your Inbox and tasks linked to items in your containers.

You can also view tasks at container level to see everything that needs doing within that container.

When viewing tasks on an item, you can choose whether to include tasks linked to its child items.

## How do reminders reach me?

Reminders are sent by email. Under **Settings → Notifications**, you can choose which notifications you want to receive and configure quiet hours when no notifications are sent.

## Can I see my tasks in my own calendar?

Yes. Each container has a personal **Calendar** link that you can subscribe to in Google Calendar, Apple Calendar, Outlook or any other calendar app that supports calendar subscriptions.

Tasks with an assigned date appear in your calendar. Recurring tasks are added as they become due or when the previous occurrence has been handled, rather than generating an endless series of future events.

The calendar only shows tasks and information you have access to. Calendar subscriptions are free on every plan.
