## 6. How we use cookies and analytics

Mimers uses strictly necessary cookies for authentication, session management and security.

We do not use cookies for advertising, marketing or behavioural tracking.

We do not use third-party analytics or advertising services.

Anonymous usage statistics are collected through our own application without cookies or personal identifiers. These statistics are aggregated by day and subscription plan. Small groups are combined to reduce the possibility of identifying individual users.

We use Sentry for technical error monitoring. Error reports are filtered to remove or minimise personal information, including IP addresses, cookies, request bodies and sensitive information. User references in error reports are pseudonymised.

Sentry processes this information within the EU.

## 12. How long do we retain personal data?

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, subject to legal obligations and legitimate retention requirements.

The applicable retention period depends on the type of information.

**Account and user content**

Your account information and user content are generally retained while your account remains active.

If an account becomes inactive, we may apply the following process:
- After 12 months of inactivity, we may send an inactivity notice.
- After 15 months, the account may be placed in read-only mode.
- After 18 months, the account may be deleted.

**Technical and security records**

| Data | Retention |
|---|---|
| Registration IP address | 90 days |
| Session information, including session IP address | Session lifetime plus no more than 24 hours |
| Email and password change records | No later than 24 hours after becoming unusable |
| Audit log | 12 months after the relevant container has been purged or the account deleted |
| Security log | 12 months |

Audit logs may contain a user identifier associated with records of actions performed in Mimers. Their metadata is limited to structured information such as identifiers, types, field names, amounts and levels.

**Trash and exports**

Deleted content placed in the trash is retained for up to 30 days before permanent deletion.

Generated exports are retained for up to 7 days. An account export is retained for 24 hours.

Temporary signed file delivery links expire after 15 minutes.

**Legal retention requirements**

Certain information may need to be retained for longer where required by law, to comply with legal obligations or to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

Where a valid legal preservation requirement applies, deletion may be delayed for the information covered by that requirement.

**Backups**

Information that has been deleted from the active service may remain in backups for a limited period before it is permanently removed.

## 13. Account deletion

You can request deletion of your account through **Settings → Security**.

The deletion process requires confirmation through a link sent to your registered email address. The link is valid for one hour. If two-factor authentication is enabled, a valid authentication code is also required when requesting deletion.

Once the deletion link is used, the deletion process is initiated. There is no additional cancellation period.

When an account is deleted:
- Your account and personal account information are removed.
- Your personal data is deleted or anonymised where appropriate.
- You lose access to containers shared with you, in accordance with the applicable access and ownership rules.
- Content in shared containers may be affected by the deletion, and other members may be notified.
- Audit and security logs may remain for their applicable retention periods.
- Information subject to a valid legal preservation requirement may be retained for as long as necessary.

Deleting your account does not delete content in containers owned by other accounts.

## 14. Your rights under GDPR

Where GDPR applies to our processing of your personal data, you have several rights.

**Right of access**

You can request confirmation of whether we process personal data about you and obtain a copy of that information.

Information available in your Mimers account or through an account or container export does not necessarily constitute a complete copy of all personal data we process.

**Right to rectification**

You can correct inaccurate personal data.

Many account settings and user-provided records can be changed directly in Mimers. Some account information, such as your email address, is updated through a separate verification process.

**Right to erasure**

You can request deletion of personal data where the conditions under GDPR are met.

You can also delete your account using the process described in section 13.

We may need to retain certain information where a legal obligation or other lawful exception applies.

**Right to restriction of processing**

In certain circumstances, you can request that we restrict the processing of your personal data.

**Right to object**

Where we process personal data based on legitimate interests, you may have the right to object to that processing.

We will assess your objection in accordance with applicable data protection law.

**Right to data portability**

Where the right to data portability applies, you may request the personal data you have provided to us in a structured, commonly used and machine-readable format, or request that it be transmitted to another controller where technically feasible.

You can download the content you have added to Mimers, and the data we hold about your account, yourself with **Export account** in your profile settings.

**How to exercise your rights**

To exercise your rights or ask questions about how we process personal data, contact us through the [contact form](/contact) on the Mimers website.

We may need to verify your identity before processing a request.

We will respond within the time limits required by applicable data protection law.
