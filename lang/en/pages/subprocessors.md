This page lists the subprocessors engaged by Mimers: service providers that process personal data on behalf of Mimers to provide the service. The list is updated when providers or processing arrangements change.

| Provider | Purpose | Data and processing scope | Processing location |
|---|---|---|---|
| Inleed | Hosting and infrastructure | Application database, stored files and operational logs | Sweden and France (EU) |
| Mailgun | Email delivery | Recipient email address and email content needed to deliver service messages | EU region |
| Sentry | Technical error monitoring | Filtered and pseudonymised technical error reports | EU region |

Paddle is the Merchant of Record for all orders and handles payments under its own terms. It is not a subprocessor and is described in our [Privacy Policy](/privacy).
