If Mimers Pro or Pro+ is not right for you, you can get your money back within 30 days of paying. No questions asked.

## 1. 30-day money-back guarantee

Every payment for Mimers Pro or Pro+ can be refunded in full if you ask within 30 days of the date you were charged. This applies to:

- the first payment for a subscription, including a subscription at the founder price;
- each renewal of a subscription, counted from the date of that renewal.

You do not need to give a reason. We refund the full amount you paid, including any tax.

This guarantee is in addition to your rights under consumer law, including the right of withdrawal that Paddle handles as Merchant of Record. It does not replace or limit them.

## 2. How to ask for a refund

Our order process is conducted by our online reseller Paddle.com. Paddle.com is the Merchant of Record for all our orders and handles returns. You can ask for a refund in either of two ways:

- **Through Paddle** — use the link in your receipt email, or look up your purchase at [paddle.net](https://paddle.net).
- **Through us** — send a message through the [contact form](/contact) under *Billing & Subscription*, from the account the subscription belongs to or with the email address on the receipt, and we will pass the request on to Paddle.

Refunds go back to the payment method you used. Paddle usually processes them within a few working days; how soon the money shows up depends on your bank or card provider.

## 3. What happens to your account

A refund ends the Pro or Pro+ time that payment paid for, and the refunded subscription is cancelled so it does not renew. Your account stays and moves to the Free plan, in the same way as when a subscription ends.

If your account then uses more storage than the Free plan allows, it becomes read-only and you have three months to remove files, export your data or subscribe again before content is removed. The details are in section 8 of our [Terms of Use](/terms).

If you paid the founder price, you keep it if you subscribe again within those three months. After that, the founder price is lost.

A refund is not a request to delete your data. Your account and data follow the ordinary retention rules in our [Privacy Policy](/privacy). To delete them, delete your account.

If you delete your account, your subscription ends without a refund. If you want your money back, ask for the refund before you delete your account.

## 4. Cancelling without a refund

You can cancel your subscription at any time with **Manage billing** on your profile or through Paddle. Cancelling stops future renewals; you keep Pro or Pro+ until the end of the period you have paid for. If you are still within 30 days of your last payment and want your money back as well, ask for a refund as described above.

## 5. After 30 days

After 30 days, payments are not normally refunded. Your rights under mandatory consumer law are not affected. If something went wrong on our side — a double charge, or the service did not work as described — contact us through the [contact form](/contact) and we will put it right.
