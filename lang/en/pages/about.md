## Structure on your terms.

Life comes with a lot of things to keep track of. The things we own, the projects we manage, the responsibilities we carry and all the information that comes with them.

Yet, keeping everything organised often means using several different tools, spreadsheets, notes and reminders that never quite fit together.

**Mimers brings it all together.**

Mimers is a flexible information and organisation platform that helps you keep track of the things you own, use or work with. From a boat, car or home to a business, customer or project, Mimers gives you one place to organise information, documents, tasks, maintenance and history.

## Born out of a real need

Mimers started with a simple problem: keeping track of where things were stored on a boat.

A spreadsheet could do the job, but it wasn't particularly smart, flexible or useful for managing everything else that came with boat ownership.

The idea gradually grew into something bigger. We wanted a single system that could combine the best parts of information management, knowledge organisation and task management — without the limitations of having to switch between several different tools.

What started as a personal solution soon revealed a much broader need. People and businesses face similar challenges every day, regardless of what they own, manage or work with.

That's why we decided to make Mimers available to everyone.

## One system. Many possibilities.

Mimers doesn't need to know whether you're organising a boat, managing a car, maintaining a property, running a business or coordinating a project.

You decide how to use it.

Organise information around the things that matter to you. Connect related objects, keep track of activities, build history and create workflows that suit the way you work.

Whether you're managing your personal belongings or coordinating work across a small business, the same principles apply.

Mimers provides the structure. You decide what goes into it and how it works for you.

## Information should have a life beyond ownership

Information about an object or project shouldn't disappear when ownership changes or a responsibility comes to an end.

When you sell a car, its service history and documentation should be available to the next owner. When a project is completed, the customer should be able to take over its documentation, history and relevant information.

Mimers makes it possible to transfer ownership and information while allowing previous owners or contributors to retain full or limited access when needed.

And your information should never be locked into a platform.

You can export your data, whether you want a complete copy of a container or a PDF binder for printing, sharing or safekeeping.

**Your information remains yours.**

## Powerful, without the clutter

Flexibility shouldn't come at the expense of simplicity.

We believe a good system should provide the tools you need without overwhelming you with features you don't.

Mimers is designed to grow with your needs. Advanced capabilities are there when you need them, while the everyday experience remains clean and straightforward.

We also believe technology should work for people, not the other way around. That's why Mimers supports AI agents that can interact with your information and help you get things done without having to navigate every part of the system yourself.

Our ambition isn't to be the best at everything. It's to be genuinely useful at the things that matter most.

## A name rooted in wisdom

Our name comes from Mímir, the wise giant in Norse mythology who guards Mímisbrunnr — the Well of Wisdom. A fitting inspiration for a place built around knowledge, information and the things that matter to you.

Mimers App is our take on making that wisdom useful in everyday life.
