- Delete content or otherwise reduce your storage usage to within the applicable limits.

If your account still exceeds its Free plan limits after three months, we may remove content to bring the account within those limits.

Removal may take place in the following order:

1. Attachments in the trash, starting with the oldest deleted attachments.
2. Active attachments in your own containers, starting with the newest attachments.
3. Items in the trash, starting with the oldest deleted items.
4. Active items in your own containers, starting with the newest items.

This process only applies to content in containers that you own.

If content in another user's container is billed to your account, responsibility for that storage may be transferred to the container owner's account instead. Such content will not be deleted solely because your subscription has ended.

Once your account is within the Free plan limits, normal Free plan access will be restored.

You are responsible for exporting and retaining copies of any information you wish to preserve before content is removed.
