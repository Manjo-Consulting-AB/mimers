Keeping a boat in good shape is mostly about small habits repeated often. Rinse the hull after every trip and let it dry before you cover it. Check the lines for wear and replace them before they fail. Store the boat out of direct sunlight when it is not in use.

A kayak needs a little more care than a rowing boat because the hull is thinner. Carry it rather than dragging it over stones, and rinse the inside after salt water. A cockpit cover keeps rain and leaves out between trips.

At the end of the season, wash everything with fresh water, dry it completely and hang the paddles so the blades do not warp.
