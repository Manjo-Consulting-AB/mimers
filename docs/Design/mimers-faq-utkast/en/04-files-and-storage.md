# Files and storage

## What can I upload?

You can upload almost any file type, including photos, PDFs, manuals, receipts and documents.

You can attach files to items or upload them to your Inbox and link them to an item later.

## How big can a file be?

The maximum file size depends on your plan:

- **Free:** 10 MB per file.
- **Pro:** 64 MB per file.
- **Pro+:** 64 MB per file.

## How much storage space do I have?

Your storage allowance depends on your plan:

- **Free:** 1 GB.
- **Pro:** 10 GB.
- **Pro+:** 50 GB.

Everything stored in your account counts towards your storage allowance, including both attachments and the information you enter in Mimers.

You can see your storage usage on your dashboard and on each container overview. Select the information icon next to your storage usage to see a breakdown of what's using your space.

There is no fixed limit on the number of items you can create. Only the amount of data you store determines how much of your allowance you use.

## Who pays for the storage in a shared container?

The account that creates an item is responsible for the storage it uses, regardless of who owns the container.

For example, if someone invites you to their container and you add an item or upload an attachment, the storage used by that data counts towards your own account's allowance.

This means that sharing a container doesn't automatically make the owner responsible for everyone else's storage usage.

## Does uploading the same file twice use double the space?

Yes. Each uploaded attachment counts towards your storage allowance, even if the same file has already been uploaded elsewhere.

## What happens when I run out of storage space?

When your account reaches its storage limit, it becomes read-only. You can still view and download your information, but you can no longer create or edit data.

You can still delete items, containers and attachments to free up space, or upgrade your plan.

## Do deleted files still count towards my storage?

Yes. Everything in the trash continues to count towards your storage allowance until the trash is emptied.

You can empty the trash yourself to free up space immediately.

Each container has its own trash, where you can find everything deleted from that container. The system-wide trash brings together deleted items from all containers where you have permission to access the trash.

For security reasons, AI agents cannot empty the trash on your behalf.
