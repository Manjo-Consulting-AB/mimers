# Getting started

## What is Mimers?

Mimers helps you keep track of the things that matter to you. Store manuals, receipts, photos, maintenance records, costs and other information in one organised place.

Create a **container** for anything you want to keep track of – a boat, a house, a car, a project or even your family. Then add **items** to organise everything that belongs to it.

## What is a container?

A container is a place to organise everything related to a particular thing or context. It could represent a boat, a house, a customer, a project or anything else you want to keep track of.

A container brings all related information together and can be shared with others or exported whenever you need it.

## What is an item?

An item is anything you want to keep track of within a container. It could be a physical object, such as an engine, a heat pump or a piece of furniture, but also something less tangible, like a document, a person or an activity.

Items can contain text, photos, files, categories, tags, tasks and costs. You can also link related items together or organise them in a hierarchy, placing smaller items inside larger ones.

For example, an engine can contain its individual parts, just as the engine itself belongs to a boat.

## How do I get an account?

Mimers is currently in private beta, so you need an invite code to create an account.

You can register your interest **here** and get notified when Mimers opens for everyone.

## Is there a mobile app?

Not yet. Mimers is a web application that works in your phone's browser, so you can access your information wherever you are.

## Which languages does Mimers support?

Mimers is currently available in English. Support for additional languages is planned for the future.
